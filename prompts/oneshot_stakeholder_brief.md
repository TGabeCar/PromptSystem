## Instructions

Create a **1-page executive brief** that:

1. **Leads with "so what"** — why this matters, impact, outcome
2. **Uses plain language** — no jargon, or explain any technical terms
3. **Is scannable** — headers, bullets, short paragraphs
4. **Ends with next steps** — what happens next, who does what
5. **Optionally** — 3–5 FAQ for common objections or questions

Avoid walls of text. Be direct and actionable.
