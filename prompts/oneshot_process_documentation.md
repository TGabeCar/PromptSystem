{{PROCESS}}

## Instructions

Structure the process into:

1. **Overview** — what this process does, who owns it, when it runs
2. **Steps** — numbered, in order, each with:
   - What happens
   - Who does it
   - Inputs and outputs
   - Tools or systems used
   - Handoffs to next step
3. **Roles** — who is involved and their responsibilities
4. **Exceptions** — what-if scenarios, escalations, common failures
5. **Improvement notes** — automation opportunities, bottlenecks (optional)

Keep it clear and actionable. Someone unfamiliar should be able to follow it.
